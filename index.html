Hi! I am currently a Visiting Researcher at UC Berkeley, working under the guidance of Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka and Prof. Yi-Ting Chen. I am also pursuing my MSc at NYCU (GPA: 4.24/4.3). Previously, I graduated in the top 1% of my class at NTHU (ranked 2/65), where I received five Presidential Awards and the Outstanding Graduate honor. My research advances autonomous driving safety through heterogeneous traffic simulation, computer vision, and deep learning, with publications at top-tier international conferences. I am fortunate to be supported by the MOE Scholarship for Overseas Research, and I also serve as a Teaching Assistant for AI-related courses as well as a reviewer for IEEE Transactions on Reliability. I’d be delighted to connect if you share an interest in these areas!
