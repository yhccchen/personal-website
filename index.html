I am a research intern at Synology. I received my master's degree in Computer Science from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), where I was advised by Prof. Yi-Ting Chen. Previously, I was a visiting researcher at UC Berkeley's MSC Lab, working with Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka.

I study how autonomous systems can learn efficiently and remain robust in unfamiliar situations. My work spans datasets and benchmarks, controllable generative models, and efficient adaptation of multimodal models. I aim to link these directions in a learning loop that identifies gaps in experience, generates targeted counterfactuals, and learns from failures. My work includes first-author papers at ECCV and ICRA, as well as contributions to workshops at CVPR, ICCV, and ECCV.

I am seeking Fall 2027 PhD opportunities and welcome research collaborations.
